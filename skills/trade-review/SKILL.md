# Trade Review

Read [the Constitution](../../references/trading-constitution.md) and verify the journal hash chain before analysis. Never rewrite an original Thesis.

Daily: summarize decision, action, fee, slippage, error, and do-nothing counterfactual. Weekly: compare Agent, BTC buy-and-hold, fixed Grid, and Cash using P&L, drawdown, turnover, fees, win rate, and profit factor. Monthly: find regime errors, overtrading, useless inputs, and strategy drift.

Parameter changes are proposals for human approval. They may not modify Constitution, risk limits, permissions, or capital automatically.
