# Panic

Read [the Constitution](../../references/trading-constitution.md). Activate `NO_TRADE`, preserve cash, avoid reactive liquidation of Core, and surface the triggering evidence for human review. A high-severity structured news risk may veto trading; ordinary news may not create an order.

Reevaluation may shorten to 15 minutes, but only verified normalization can leave PANIC. Never widen a Grid as a substitute for stopping it.
